A flash that is interrupted - the power cut, the stick pulled - can leave the console unable to start, and installing a custom kernel voids its warranty. Keep the console powered and the stick in until it reboots by itself. Continue at your own risk.=Dette værktøj skriver til konsollens flash-hukommelse.
